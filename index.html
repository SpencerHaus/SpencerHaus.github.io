The founders

Built by two working artists.

Lauren and Cyrus have spent their careers in rehearsal rooms, audition lines and borrowed studios. They are performers based in Los Angeles who work and teach locally as well as across the country.

Spencer Haus is the place they wanted the whole time: somewhere in the Valley to train, film, record and find your people, without going over the hill.

Why Lauren is building it

“I know I’ve often needed a place like this in my career. Somewhere to be able to meet up with other creatives and unwind. Somewhere to play and work alongside other like-minded people. Somewhere to nourish the whole creative self all in one place. I want to be able to create that home for myself and my community.”

The Spencer Haus project

Part café. Part studio. Built for working artists.

A neighborhood clubhouse for actors, dancers, podcasters, filmmakers and performers. A space for multiple creative outlets.

  • Dance floor

    Sprung floor, mirrors and barres for class and rehearsal.

  • Self-tape

    Lit and ready, so the audition is the only thing you prep.

  • Scene work

    A quiet spot to run lines with a partner.

  • Podcast

    A table and mics to record a conversation.

  • Photography

    Clean light and a backdrop for headshots and shoots.

  • Café

    Good coffee and a table to work at between calls.
